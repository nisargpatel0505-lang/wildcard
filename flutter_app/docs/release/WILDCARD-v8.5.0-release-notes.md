# WILDCARD v8.5.0 release notes

## Highlights

- Rebuilt the Royal Vault with original layered Wood, Gold and Cosmetic art,
  native opening animation, rarity scan and reward reveal.
- Permanent Joker discovery is now starter/comeback/Vault only; direct
  permanent Joker purchases were removed.
- Added duplicate-protected, truthful live Vault probabilities.
- Held the experimental three-card Arcade prototype out of the shipped phone
  navigation while retaining its isolated source for possible future work.
- Added five Weekly Missions, a fair daily streak, 41 long-term tiers and
  Endless milestones.
- Extended all equipped UI themes across every registered game surface.
- Added WILD and measured high-impact shop-spacing guardrails.
- Rebalanced Sly's Stake Contract from a skill-scaled coin leak to a bounded
  risk curve verified across 4,500 deterministic runs.
- Improved the between-Heat shop's phone layout and safe-area behaviour.
- Clarified Remove Forced Ads: optional rewarded adverts remain optional.
- Added strategic run, shop, collection, Joker and Royal Vault evidence.

## Compatibility

Existing coins, unlocks, cosmetics, achievements and active save data are
preserved. The APK is still an internal release candidate until its final
phone and Google Play validation gates pass.

## Known review item

The 8,500-coin pack materially accelerates duplicate-protected Joker
collection. It does not choose Jokers or alter scoring/RNG, but it must not be
marketed as having zero gameplay impact. Product size/value should be reviewed
before public launch.
