# WILDCARD live-theme artwork — 9.1.1

Mode: built-in image generation, four independent original background plates.
No existing character, purchased skin or logo was edited. Original PNGs remain
under the local Codex generated-images folder; final project assets are below.
Mechanical conversion: FFmpeg libwebp, quality 84, compression level 6, original
dimensions preserved. Motion is native Flutter code, not baked video.

## Final prompt set

All four prompts used `stylized-concept`, a premium portrait 9:20 mobile poker
game environment rather than a UI mockup. Each required full-bleed artwork,
upper-quarter/edge detail, quieter middle/lower space for phone controls, and
no text, logos, watermarks, people, cards, coins, foreground table or UI buttons.

### Midnight Observatory

An elegant Art Deco observatory casino high above clouds at night, fine brushed
champagne brass and deep-blue lapis arches. An enormous astronomical armillary
sphere high in the upper quarter, luminous icy-blue crescent and faint aurora
beyond a domed glass roof; tiny card-suit etchings in metal. Engraved brass,
dark-blue velvet, smoky glass, aqua accents; crisp authored concept art, not
noisy texture. Low-contrast midnight-blue middle/lower two-thirds, distant
balcony and dim smooth floor. Readable jewel tones, no busy central ornament,
motion blur or dense particles. Native aurora ribbons and gold star motes are
rendered separately.

Asset: `flutter_app/assets/art/backgrounds/wildcard-theme-midnight-observatory.webp`

### Jade Conservatory

An exquisite Art Deco botanical card lounge: lush jade/emerald ferns around a
tall curved glass conservatory, softly illuminated white water lilies and a
distant circular moon gate in the upper quarter. Champagne-brass ribs, cream
stone and filtered honey morning light; tranquil luxury, card-suit motifs
engraved into brass edges. Painterly realism and refined textures, rich green
and pale gold distinct from a dark castle. Quiet central/lower dark-jade floor
and calm pool reflections; no dense central flowers. Native fireflies and
leaf-light movement are rendered separately.

Asset: `flutter_app/assets/art/backgrounds/wildcard-theme-jade-conservatory.webp`

### Neon Afterhours

An ultramodern private card lounge above a rainy futuristic city, smoked-black
glass, curved panoramic window, turquoise skyline and magenta architectural
reflections. A hollow diamond-shaped cyan neon sculpture integrated into the
upper-left ceiling. Refined luxury, not arcade clutter; electric cyan, orchid
magenta, ink violet and cool silver. Crisp dimensional concept art. Skyline
and focal detail in upper third and side edges; spacious low-contrast plum
middle/bottom with smooth floor reflections. No heavy bloom, chromatic
aberration, grain or signage. Native rain glints and neon light sweeps are
rendered separately.

Asset: `flutter_app/assets/art/backgrounds/wildcard-theme-neon-afterhours.webp`

### Crimson Theatre

An opulent intimate Art Deco magic theatre and secret card salon, oxblood
velvet curtains at the extreme edges, carved rose-gold balconies, scalloped
brass ceiling ornaments and softly lit empty stage arch with subtle heart and
spade carvings high in the composition. Amber footlights, ruby burgundy, warm
black shadows, fine velvet and brushed metal. Lavish but restrained original
concept art. Quiet middle/lower wine-red stage floor and gentle ruby gradients;
no intrusive props, dense particles or baked light streaks. Native spotlight
arcs and sparse golden dust are rendered separately.

Asset: `flutter_app/assets/art/backgrounds/wildcard-theme-crimson-theatre.webp`
